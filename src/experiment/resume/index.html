Environmental Degradation Monitor

Sep 2025 – Jun 2026

Trained and evaluated a U-Net segmentation model for satellite land-cover classification, then built a Streamlit application around it: on-demand Sentinel-2 retrieval, tiled inference, mask-based change detection, and intersection with protected-area boundaries on an interactive map.

When applied to real imagery from the target region, the model’s accuracy collapsed — it had learned a different geography. I diagnosed the domain shift and scoped the fix, rather than shipping a plausible-looking but unreliable result.

  • TensorFlow / Keras
  • Sentinel-2
  • geopandas
  • Streamlit
