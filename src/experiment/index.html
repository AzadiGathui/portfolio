How I work

Start from the decision.

Before a chart or a chapter, I ask who will act on this and what they need to be sure of. The page is structured around that answer — not around the order the research happened in.

Colour means something, or it isn’t used.

On this page red marks only the signal: the species at risk, the place your eye should go. An accent spent everywhere is an accent spent nowhere.

Accessibility is where craft starts.

An accessibility conference needs a booklet everyone can read; a fintech app for small traders needs Kiswahili. Constraints like these make the work sharper for everyone else, too.
