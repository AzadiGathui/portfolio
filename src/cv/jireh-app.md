Worked from PM-defined acceptance criteria through to engineering handoff.

- Redesigned signup, cutting median signup time from 5.2 to 2.1 mins (−59%).
- Led the modernisation of Jireh Health’s frontend design system:
  - introduced reusable primitives, semantic tokens, and consistent UI patterns
  - improved scalability and maintainability across mobile and web.
- Developed an in-house prototyping tool that:
  - mirrors the deployed frontend
  - decouples UX testing from backend dependencies
