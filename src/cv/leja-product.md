- Owned onboarding, referral, and transaction-flow design end-to-end: research, design, developer handoff, and iteration based on usage.
- Designed for a low-bandwidth, non-technical, non-English-dominant user base.
- Partnered directly with developers and product managers to ship designs to real users, then iterated based on usability testing and product feedback.
- Formally led a 2-person design/research team on a dedicated initiative, assigning work and owning outcomes while remaining hands-on in the craft.
- Designed interactive prototypes for partnership presentations and product demos using Figma and the Adobe suite.
- Championed accessibility and usability against W3C/WCAG 2.1 guidelines; produced clear design documentation to ease developer handoff.
