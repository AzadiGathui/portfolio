Children & Youth Pavilion Report COP27

Post-event report for COP27

ClientChildren's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF)
IndustryClimate & Youth Advocacy
RoleEditorial Designer
DeliverablesPost-event report — layout, typesetting and colour system
Behind the scenes

Using the existing brand to determine structure & layout.

With the colour palette and typeface already decided by the team that did the branding, I worked to typeset the text on a precise grid in Adobe InDesign.

I harmonised the colours into a logical scheme, grouped by thematic area and content type i.e. health was yellow, recommendations were blue etc.
