Youth & Climate

Children & Youth Pavilion at COP27

Report design for the Children & Youth Pavilion at COP27 in Sharm el-Sheikh, built on the pavilion's existing brand and typeset on a precise InDesign grid.

Client
Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF)
Role
Editorial Designer
Deliverables
Post-event report: layout, typesetting and colour system
Behind the scenes

Using the existing brand to determine structure & layout.

With the colour palette and typeface already decided by the team that did the branding, I worked to typeset the text on a precise grid in Adobe InDesign.

I harmonised the colours into a logical scheme, grouped by thematic area and content type i.e. health was yellow, recommendations were blue etc.
